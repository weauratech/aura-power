== Next Steps ==

  1. Opt-in workloads for power management:
     kubectl annotate deployment <name> aura.sh/power-eligible=true

  2. Or opt-in an entire namespace:
     kubectl annotate namespace <name> aura.sh/power-eligible=true

  3. Create your first policy:
     kubectl apply -f - <<EOF
     apiVersion: power.aura.sh/v1alpha1
     kind: PowerPolicy
     metadata:
       name: dev-off-hours
       namespace: {{ .Release.Namespace }}
     spec:
       scope:
         namespaces: [dev]
       schedule:
         desiredState: "on"
         windows:
           - start: "08:00"
             end: "18:00"
             days: [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
             timezone: "UTC"
       priority: 10
     EOF

  Documentation: https://github.com/weauratech/aura-power
